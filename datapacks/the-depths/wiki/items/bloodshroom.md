# Bloodshroom

## Event

On kill monster.

## Effect

Heal player by `<n>` hearts where `<n>` is the killed monster's `bloodiness` value.

`bloodiness` should be high if the monster looks like it would have a lot of blood.

E.g. Skeletons should have a `bloodiness` of zero.

Bosses have a considerably higher `bloodiness` value.
