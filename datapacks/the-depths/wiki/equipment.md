# Equipment

## General

### Tier

An item's tier describes on which level you will likely find this.

- Tier 0 are starter items.
- Tier 1 can be commonly found on Level 1.
- Tier 2 can rarely be found on Level 1 and commonly on Level 2.
- Tier 3 can rarely be found on Level 2 and commonly on Level 3.
- etc...

Item names are colored in the same way as the level of their tier.
